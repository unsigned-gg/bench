02 / Methodology

The limit belongs
beside the number.

Useful benchmarks make their conditions legible. Three evidence types, each answering a different question.

01

Measured

A local executable capture. Workload, percentile, sample count and environment travel with the result. Repeat on your own hardware before generalizing.

02

Simulated

A declared scheduling model on a virtual clock. Units belong to the plan; makespan describes that model rather than wall-clock execution.

03

Specified

An inspected protocol with its coverage and source identity. Numeric results appear when a corresponding execution record is attached.
