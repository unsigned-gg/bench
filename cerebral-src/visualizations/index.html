Each entry links to the original author’s example. This lab reimplements the visual concept with reusable local layouts, an orthographic Three.js renderer, and compiled Rust numeric kernels. Titles identify the source concept; values and historical-looking profiles are demonstrations.

Pan and zoom examples expose their view controls. Brush examples select a region. Hierarchical examples let you drill in and reset. Motion is opt-in and respects reduced-motion settings.
